Our Story

Founded in 1985 by a family of passionate pyrotechnicians, Classic Fireworks has grown from a small local operation into one of the region's most trusted names in professional fireworks displays. What began as backyard shows for neighborhood celebrations quickly evolved into full-scale productions for cities, corporations, and couples across the country.

With over four decades of experience, our team combines time-honored artistry with cutting-edge technology to create displays that leave audiences speechless. Every show is custom-designed, safety-certified, and choreographed to deliver maximum impact — because we believe every event deserves its own moment of magic.
